The Brief

B2B lead gen for a career fair.

Jobbio needed businesses to register as exhibitors for their career fair. The goal was signed exhibitor agreements, not just clicks or traffic. Every lead had to be worth chasing.

At €2,250 average deal value, quality mattered more than volume. The campaign needed to reach the right decision-makers, convert them on the ad platform itself, and then move them through a follow-up sequence to a paid commitment.

The Numbers

Why LinkedIn Forms Won

LinkedIn native Lead Gen Forms converted at 64%. The alternative, directing users to a landing page, converted at 36%. Keeping users in-platform removed the single biggest source of drop-off: the redirect.

The Strategy

In-platform forms. Three-stage follow-up.

🔗

LinkedIn Native Lead Gen Forms

Forms served directly inside LinkedIn. Users register without leaving the platform, which removes the redirect, the page load, and the biggest source of conversion drop-off. The 64% CTR vs. 36% for a landing page shows the difference clearly.

🎯

Senior HR and Talent Acquisition Targeting

LinkedIn job title and function targeting to reach HR directors and talent acquisition leads at companies positioned to exhibit at a career fair. Reaching the right person first cut the cost per qualified registration significantly.

📋

3-Stage Follow-up Sequence

A structured 3-stage sequence built to convert form registrations into paid exhibitor agreements. Each stage addressed a specific objection or decision point in the buying process, moving businesses from interested to committed.
